THIS DINOSAUR WAS AT ONE OF MY FAVORITE PARKS AS A CHILD, THE PARK I DEEMED THE DINOSAUR PARK.

WHEN I WAS YOUNGER, MY DAD WOULD REGULARLY TAKE ME TO THE PARK. HE TOOK ME TO SO MANY PARKS, SO OFTEN, THAT I GAVE EACH ONE A NAME. MANY OF THESE PARKS WERE NAMED AFTER COLORS, EXCEPT FOR MY FAVORITE ONE: THE DINOSAUR PARK.

GROWING UP, I SPENT A LOT OF TIME WITH MY DAD, AND I CHERISHED THE TIME WE SPENT TOGETHER, ESPECIALLY WHEN WE WERE AT THE PARK. SPENDING TIME WITH MY DAD WAS A HIGHLIGHT OF MY CHILDHOOD. TO ME, THIS DINOSAUR NOT ONLY REPRESENTS MY FAVORITE PLACE BUT ALSO MY RELATIONSHIP WITH MY DAD.

FROM A YOUNG AGE TO NOW, I HAVE ALWAYS VALUED TIME AND MEMORIES WITH MY DAD. THE DINOSAUR PARK HOLDS MANY CHILDHOOD MEMORIES, ESPECIALLY ONES WITH MY DAD, WHICH IS WHY IT IS SENTIMENTAL TO ME.
